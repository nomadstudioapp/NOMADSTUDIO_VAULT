# Session · Miami 1986

![Before and after](before_after.jpg)

**EN** — A 1986-style TV frame, taken to 2026 in one [Nomad Studio](https://github.com/nomadstudioapp/NomadStudio) Session: outpaint 4:3 → 16:9, edit to a modern-series look with Qwen Image 2.1, and a 4x upscale with SeedVR2 (5376×3072). Everything ran locally on a 4060 Ti 16 GB. Every step, with its prompt, seed and models, is in [session.md](session.md); the same export as a .zip is in the [Release](https://github.com/nomadstudioapp/NOMADSTUDIO_VAULT/releases/tag/session-miami-1986).

**ES** — Un fotograma al estilo de una serie de 1986, llevado a 2026 en una sola Session de [Nomad Studio](https://github.com/nomadstudioapp/NomadStudio): outpaint de 4:3 a 16:9, edición a look de serie moderna con Qwen Image 2.1 y upscale 4x con SeedVR2 (5376×3072). Todo en local, en una 4060 Ti de 16 GB. Cada paso, con su prompt, semilla y modelos, está en [session.md](session.md); la misma exportación en .zip está en la [Release](https://github.com/nomadstudioapp/NOMADSTUDIO_VAULT/releases/tag/session-miami-1986).
